Secure My REP LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026  ·  Company: Secure My REP LLC  ·  State of Incorporation: Delaware

Secure My REP LLC (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) maintains this Privacy Policy to describe the collection, use, storage, and protection of personal information obtained through the delivery of advisory, consulting, and educational services. Use of any Secure My REP LLC service constitutes acceptance of this Policy.

1. Information Collected

Secure My REP LLC collects only information necessary to deliver contracted services. This includes:

  • Full name and contact information, including email address and telephone number
  • Business or organizational information voluntarily disclosed during advisory, consulting, or educational sessions
  • Scheduling, correspondence, and session-related communications
  • Payment transaction records processed exclusively by Stripe, Inc. Secure My REP LLC does not store, transmit, or have access to payment card data

2. Use of Information

Information collected is used solely to: (a) deliver and administer advisory, consulting, and educational services; (b) schedule and confirm sessions; (c) communicate with clients regarding active engagements; and (d) satisfy applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations. Information is not used for third-party advertising, behavioral profiling, or any purpose unrelated to service delivery.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal information is processed on the following legal bases: (a) performance of a contract, to deliver services purchased by the client; and (b) legitimate business interests, including maintenance of engagement records and compliance with applicable law.

4. Third-Party Processors and Data Sharing

Secure My REP LLC does not sell, rent, license, or trade personal information under any circumstances. Disclosure to third parties is limited to: (a) Stripe, Inc., for payment processing, governed by Stripe’s Privacy Policy available at stripe.com/privacy; (b) service providers operating under written confidentiality obligations and engaged solely to support service delivery; and (c) governmental or law enforcement authorities when disclosure is required by applicable law, court order, or legal process.

5. Data Security

Secure My REP LLC applies administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with industry standards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Data is encrypted in transit. In the event of a breach materially affecting client data, Secure My REP LLC will provide notification as required under applicable law.

6. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for the duration necessary to fulfill contracted services and satisfy applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations. Upon written request, Secure My REP LLC will delete or anonymize personal information unless retention is required by law.

7. Client Rights

Clients retain the following rights with respect to their personal information:

  • Right of access: the right to request a copy of personal information held by Secure My REP LLC
  • Right of deletion: the right to request deletion, subject to applicable legal retention requirements
  • Right of correction: the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records
  • California residents: the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. Secure My REP LLC does not sell personal information

To exercise any of the above rights, submit a written request to support@securemyrep.com with subject line “Privacy Request,” including full name and description of the request. Requests will be acknowledged within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Secure My REP LLC does not currently operate a dedicated website. Upon launch of any web property, a full disclosure of cookies, analytics tools, and tracking technologies will be incorporated into this Policy. No targeted advertising or third-party tracking is currently employed.

9. Minors

Services are offered exclusively to individuals aged 18 and older. Secure My REP LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from minors. Any such data identified will be deleted promptly.

10. Policy Updates

Secure My REP LLC reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be communicated to active clients by email no fewer than 14 days before taking effect. Continued use of services following the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law provisions.
