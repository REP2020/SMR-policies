Refund Policy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026  ·  Company: Secure My REP  ·  State of Incorporation: Delaware

Secure My REP provides professional advisory, consulting, and educational services. The following terms govern all purchases of Secure My REP services.

1. All Sales Final

Due to the professional, time-reserved, and preparation-intensive nature of advisory services, all purchases are final and non-refundable once a session is scheduled. By completing payment and confirming a session, the client acknowledges and agrees to this Policy in full.

2. Rescheduling

Clients may submit one rescheduling request per purchased session, provided the request is received by email at support@securemyrep.com no fewer than 24 hours before the scheduled session start time. Rescheduling is subject to availability. Rescheduling requests submitted with fewer than 24 hours notice will not be honored.

3. Missed Sessions

Failure to attend a scheduled session without prior written notice submitted in accordance with Section 2 constitutes a forfeiture of that session. No credit, reschedule, or alternative remedy will be provided for missed sessions.

4. Cancellation by Secure My REP

In the event Secure My REP is unable to deliver a scheduled session, Secure My REP will reschedule the session to a mutually agreed date at no additional charge. Rescheduling under this section is the client’s sole and exclusive remedy. This provision satisfies Secure My REP’s obligation to deliver contracted services and no further obligation, monetary or otherwise, shall arise from a Company-initiated cancellation.

5. Extraordinary Circumstances

Requests for consideration arising from documented extraordinary circumstances may be submitted to support@securemyrep.com within 7 days of the scheduled session date. Any determination is at the sole and absolute discretion of Secure My REP. Submission of a request does not create any obligation to provide accommodation, and no precedent is established by any prior determination.

6. Governing Law

This Refund Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law provisions. Disputes are subject to the dispute resolution provisions set forth in the Terms and Conditions.
